Privacy Policy

Baby Cry Monitor · Effective October 27, 2026
Baby Cry Monitor ("the app") alerts a connected parent phone when the phone placed near your baby (the baby phone) detects a loud sound. No sign-up is needed, and the app never asks for your name, phone number or email.

1. Information we process

  • Detected sound (10 seconds): when "Send detected sound" is on (on by default), about 10 seconds of audio right before a detection is compressed and uploaded. Only the connected parent phone can play it, and it is deleted automatically after 24 hours. Turn the setting off and no audio is uploaded.
  • Loudness check: all other microphone input is measured for loudness on the baby phone and discarded at once. It is never recorded to a file or sent to a server.
  • Detection log: the type and time of detections and of monitoring start/stop.
  • Connection data: the baby name (or nickname) you enter, the device model of both phones, an anonymous ID created by the app, push notification tokens, the parent phone's language setting, and a one-time 6-digit connection code.
  • Baby phone status: monitoring on/off, battery level and charging, microphone availability, last seen time.
  • Security log: to stop brute-force guessing of connection codes, connection attempts are counted per anonymous ID and IP address for 10 minutes.
  • Crash data: device model, OS/app version and error details when the app crashes (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Usage statistics: the alert method chosen when monitoring starts (parent phone alert, vibration or sound), screen views and app sessions, an anonymous analytics ID created by the app, approximate region derived from the IP address (city level; the IP address itself is not stored), and Premium purchase events (product and price) (Firebase Analytics).
  • Purchase data: whether Premium was purchased (store receipt). The app never receives card or payment details.

The baby photo stays on the baby phone and is never uploaded. The app does not collect contacts, photos, precise location or advertising IDs, and shows no ads.

3. Service providers

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. We use:

  • Google Firebase — anonymous authentication, storage of connection data, detection logs and detected sound (Realtime Database), connection handling (Cloud Functions), push notifications (Cloud Messaging; via Apple Push Notification service on iOS), crash reports (Crashlytics) and usage statistics (Analytics). Data may be stored on Google servers outside your country (e.g. the US).
  • Google Play / App Store — Premium payment and purchase verification.

4. Retention

  • Detected sound: deleted after 24 hours.
  • Detection log: deleted after 7 days.
  • Usage statistics: deleted after 2 months (Google Analytics retention setting).
  • Connection code: deleted when used or expired.
  • Security log (attempt count, IP address): deleted after 10 minutes (cleanup runs hourly).
  • Parent phone data: deleted when you disconnect or switch to another parent phone.
  • Baby name, device model and baby phone status: kept while the connection exists, and deleted automatically, together with any remaining detection log and sound, when the baby phone has not been used for 90 days. Email us to have them deleted sooner.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone (baby phone, required): loudness detection and the 10-second sound.
  • Notifications (optional): alerts. Without them, pairing and in-app alerts still work; you just won't get alerts while the app is closed.
  • Camera (optional): scanning the connection QR code.
  • Photos (optional): only when choosing a baby photo. The photo never leaves the device.

You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

6. Children

The app is used by parents on their own devices and collects nothing directly from children. You may enter a nickname instead of the baby's name. Detected sound may include sounds around the baby; turn off "Send detected sound" if you prefer.

7. Your rights

You can turn off sound sending, disconnect, change permissions or delete the app at any time. Email us to access or delete data kept on the server, and we will handle it without delay.
